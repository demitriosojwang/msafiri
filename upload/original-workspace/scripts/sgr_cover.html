SGR Feeder

A multi-user transport coordination system connecting passengers arriving at or departing from the Mombasa SGR Terminus with shared and private cab services along the North Coast and South Coast corridors.

This document defines the production architecture, domain model, database schema, booking state machine, M-Pesa payment flows, location tracking strategy, and security model.
